Methodology CHAINED JEVONS
Year-over-year price shifts are compared using an unweighted geometric mean of price ratios for items purchased in both years (Ω), then chain-linked across the full 2022–2026 span so no single year's basket composition dominates the index.
I(t) = I(t−1) × ∏i∈Ω ( P(i,t) / P(i,t−1) )1/n
Budget weights (page 02) size each category's contribution to total spend but do not enter the Jevons ratio itself — the index is a pure price signal, decoupled from how much of each category you actually buy.
